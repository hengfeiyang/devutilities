§ 01Capabilities

Six things it does cleanly.

i

Any direction

JSON, YAML, TOML, and CSV — pick any source and any target with a from/to picker and a one-click swap button.

ii

Order preserved

A shared value tree keeps object keys in the order you wrote them; JSON uses a custom order-preserving parser instead of reshuffling fields.

iii

Dates kept verbatim

Date and datetime literals carry across untouched, so a round-trip never rewrites or reformats them.

iv

Nested ↔ CSV

Nested objects and arrays flatten into dotted-key columns (address.city, tags.0) with an ordered union header; reading CSV rebuilds the nesting.

v

CSV type inference

An Infer types toggle coerces cells like 123, true, and 98.5 into numbers and booleans — or keeps every cell as a string.

vi

Real YAML

YAML parses and serializes through the Yams library, preserving key order and quoting rather than guessing.

§ 04Questions, answered

Things people ask before they download.

Does it keep my key order?

Yes. Objects are stored as ordered key/value pairs end to end, and JSON is parsed with a custom order-preserving scanner — so fields come out in the order you wrote them, not alphabetized or reshuffled.

How is nested data handled in CSV?

Nested objects and arrays flatten to dotted-key columns — address.city, tags.0, friends.0.id — and the header is the ordered union of every row's columns. Reading a CSV reverses this, rebuilding objects and arrays from the dotted keys.

Will CSV numbers become real numbers?

Your choice. The Infer types toggle coerces cells like 123, true, and 98.5 into numbers and booleans (empty cells become null), or you can keep every cell as a string.

What about dates?

Date and datetime literals are carried verbatim. TOML date literals stay unquoted; nothing gets reformatted on a round-trip.

Is the YAML support real?

Yes — parsing and serialization go through the Yams library via its node API, which preserves key order and quoting and handles the awkward corners of the spec.

Does anything leave my machine?

No. Conversion is synchronous and entirely local. Nothing is uploaded.
