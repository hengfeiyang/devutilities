§ 04Questions, answered

Things people ask before they download.

Which error-correction levels are supported?

L (7%), M (15%), Q (25%), and H (30%). Higher levels recover from more physical damage at the cost of code density.

Can I overlay a logo?

The tool generates the bare code; you can overlay a logo in your image editor. Use level Q or H so the overlay doesn't break decoding.

What can it decode?

Any standard QR code in PNG / JPG / TIFF / common image formats. Drag the image into the scan area.

Does it support coloured QRs?

Generated codes are black on white for maximum scanner compatibility. Colour overlays are best handled in an image editor.

Does this require network access?

No. Generation and scanning are fully local.

Maximum capacity?

Standard QR limits — up to about 4296 alphanumeric characters at level L. Practical limits depend on density and EC.
