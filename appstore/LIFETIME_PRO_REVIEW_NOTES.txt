DevUtilities Lifetime Pro is a one-time Non-Consumable In-App Purchase.
Product ID: com.hengfeiyang.devutilities.pro.lifetime
It permanently unlocks AI Chat, AI Translate, Parquet, IP Lookup, Currency,
JWT, and Crypto. There is no recurring subscription or automatic renewal.
The other 18 tools remain free. Eligible purchases support Family Sharing.

HOW TO REVIEW
1. Launch DevUtilities 3.0. No DevUtilities account or sign-in is required.
2. Click the crown at the top of the sidebar, or choose
   DevUtilities > DevUtilities Pro... from the menu bar.
3. The Pro window lists all seven tools. It offers Start 30-Day Trial,
   Unlock Pro Forever with the localized App Store price, and Restore Purchase.
4. Choose Unlock Pro Forever to start Apple's purchase flow. Starting the
   trial first is not required. A verified successful purchase unlocks all
   seven tools immediately and shows Lifetime Pro status.
5. Restore Purchase uses Apple's restore flow for the same Apple Account.
   Once permanent Pro is active, purchase prompts are hidden; the sidebar
   crown remains available to view the entitlement.

EARLY SUPPORTERS AND SANDBOX
Verified original App Store acquisitions from January 1, 2025 through
October 24, 2026 receive permanent Pro, whether originally paid or free.
The precise eligible interval is [2025-01-01 00:00, 2026-10-25 00:00)
in Asia/Shanghai. This is based on AppTransaction.originalPurchaseDate,
not first launch. Existing eligible customers do not need to buy again.
Sandbox's fixed original acquisition date in 2013 is outside this interval,
so it does not trigger the Early Supporter grant. Sandbox review can use
the ordinary trial and purchase screen without a special review-only mode.
A Sandbox account with an existing Lifetime Pro purchase must use a fresh
test account or reset its test purchase history to review the purchase flow.

TRIAL AND TOOL ACCESS
The optional 30-day trial is app-managed and starts only when the user chooses
Start 30-Day Trial. No purchase or recurring charge is created. After expiry,
selecting a locked Pro tool opens this same purchase window while retaining
the previously selected free tool. There is no daily usage allowance.

AI FEATURES
AI Chat and AI Translate require the user's own provider credentials in
Settings > AI > Providers. Lifetime Pro does not include external API credits.
JWT, Crypto, and Parquet can demonstrate Pro access without an AI key.
macOS speech does not require an OpenAI key. Optional OpenAI read-aloud uses
AI-generated speech and does not capture microphone audio.
